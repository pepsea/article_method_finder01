INCLUDE a paper if it is primary research (or a method/validation paper) in which nicotine and/or its metabolites (cotinine, trans-3'-hydroxycotinine, nornicotine, anabasine, nicotine-N-oxide, etc.) are measured or quantified using LC-MS, LC-MS/MS or HPLC-MS/MS, in any matrix (biofluids, tissue, hair, e-liquid, tobacco, air, water, food). Method development, validation, comparison of methods, and applied studies that describe the LC-MS(/MS) measurement all count.

EXCLUDE a paper if any of the following holds:
- Nicotine or its metabolites are not analysed by mass spectrometry (for example only immunoassay, GC-only, ELISA, questionnaire or self-report of smoking).
- Mass spectrometry is used, but not to measure nicotine or its metabolites (for example proteomics or other drugs in a smoking-related population).
- It is a narrative review, editorial, comment, or letter without original measurement data.

Use UNSURE only when the abstract is missing or too short to decide, or when it is genuinely ambiguous whether nicotine/metabolites were measured by LC-MS.
